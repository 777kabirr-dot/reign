Our Origin

Born deep in
the Wai region.

Our water begins as rain over the Western Ghats, then travels for years through ancient rock — filtered by the earth, enriched with minerals along the way.

It surfaces at protected underground springs, cool and perfectly balanced. We canned it where it rises, so what reaches you is exactly what nature made.
