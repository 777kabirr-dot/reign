02The Thesis

Water is the last unbranded luxury.

The wealthy have a wardrobe of luxury for every ritual — champagne, espresso, fragrance, fashion. Yet the most consumed product on earth, the one held in hand at every dinner, spa and suite, remains a plastic afterthought. ELYPHANT turns water into a status object — an aluminium vessel worthy of the table it sits on.

  • Frequency × emotion. Consumed daily, gifted, displayed — the rare luxury with everyday occasions.
  • Aluminium over glass & plastic. Lighter, infinitely recyclable, light-proof — premium and sustainable.
  • A house, not a SKU. Pure, Reserve and Noir let one brand price from accessible-luxury to ultra-premium.
  • Margin lives in perception. The liquid costs cents; the brand commands dollars.

10Supply Chain

From the spring to the suite.

Stage 01

Source

Protected aquifer, sealed catchment, zero surface contact in the Western Ghats.

Stage 02

Fill & Seal

Canned at source in light-proof aluminium — freshness locked, plastic eliminated.

Stage 03

Distribute

Temperature-stable, lightweight cases; domestic 3PL + GCC export freight forwarding.

Stage 04

Serve

Chilled, presented, poured — delivered to hotels, clubs, retail & D2C doorsteps.

  • Aluminium = logistics edge. ~40% lighter than glass, unbreakable, stackable — lower freight, zero breakage loss.
  • Dual-node ready. India for domestic + a GCC bonded warehouse to serve the Gulf without import friction.
  • Traceability. Batch-level provenance from spring to can — the transparency luxury buyers now demand.
  • Resilient sourcing. Multi-supplier aluminium & co-pack agreements remove single-point risk.

11Gross Margin Projections

Margins expand as the mix moves up.

Blended gross margin climbs from 45% at launch to 68%+ as Reserve and Noir take share and the owned line comes online.

  • Mix shift: Noir & Reserve grow from 30% → 55% of volume.
  • Scale buying: aluminium & co-pack rates fall with volume.
  • Owned line: Phase-2 capture removes co-pack margin leakage.

Built from the source up.

ELYPHANT began with a simple frustration: the most premium tables in the world still served water like an afterthought. We set out to build the brand that India — sitting on some of the planet's finest spring sources — should have created decades ago.
