## Background

Aurelia Kapoor is the founder and managing director of Kapoor Estates, a
boutique luxury real-estate developer operating across Pune and the western
Maharashtra corridor. Over fifteen years she has delivered award-winning
residential landmarks known for sustainable design and uncompromising finish.
She is a frequent speaker on green urbanism and women's leadership in Indian
real estate.
